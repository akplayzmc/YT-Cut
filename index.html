Installation

How to install

YtCut is a personal app, so it isn't in the Microsoft Store, App Store or Play Store. Your device will warn you once. That's expected, and the steps below show how to get past it.

  1. Download the installer

    Click Download for Windows above. You get one file, .

  2. Open it

    Double-click the file in your Downloads folder.

  3. Get past the SmartScreen warning

    Windows shows Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  4. Follow the installer

    Choose where to install (the default is fine) and click Install. You don't need admin rights.

  5. Start YtCut

    Open it from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut. Finished clips are saved to your Downloads folder.

Why the warning?The installer isn't code-signed with a paid Microsoft certificate, so Windows doesn't recognise the publisher yet. It appears once per PC.
UpdatingDownload the new installer and run it. It updates YtCut in place, with no need to uninstall first.
UninstallingSettings → Apps → YtCut by AK → Uninstall.
  1. Download the app

    Click Download for macOS above to get .

  2. Unzip it

    Safari usually unzips it for you. If you see the .zip in Downloads, double-click it. You'll get the YtCut app.

  3. Move YtCut into Applications

    Open Finder, then drag the YtCut app from Downloads into the Applications folder in the sidebar.

  4. Open it the first time

    Open YtCut from Applications or Launchpad. If macOS says it can't be opened or can't verify the developer, click Done or OK.

  5. Allow it in System Settings

    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to YtCut. Confirm with your password. From then on it opens normally.

Why the warning?Apple only skips the check for apps notarised through a paid Apple developer account. You only need to allow YtCut once.

Using YtCut

Videos or thumbnails in four steps

The app works the same on every device. After loading a link, pick what you want at the top: Video, Thumbnail (a full-size JPG) or Both.

01

Paste a link

Paste a video link and press Load, then choose Video, Thumbnail or Both.

02

Pick the range

Drag the slider, use the presets, or type times like 90, 1:30 or 1:02:03.

03

Choose a format

MP4 plays anywhere, MOV suits QuickTime, and MP3 or M4A save the audio only.

04

Download

Cuts are re-encoded so they start and end exactly where you chose. Short clips take seconds.

Help

Troubleshooting

Answers to the most common problems.

A video stopped loading or downloading

YouTube changes regularly, which breaks older versions of the downloader inside YtCut. Click Update yt-dlp: it's in the YtCut menu on Windows and at the top of the screen on Android. YtCut also checks for updates automatically once a week.

The video has sound but no picture

Some players can't decode every video format. Turn on Editing-friendly H.264 before downloading. H.264 plays on every device and in every editor (max 1080p).

Where are my files?

Windows and macOS: your Downloads folder. Use Show in folder after a download. Android: videos are in your Gallery (DCIM/YtCut), thumbnails too (Pictures/YtCut), and audio in Music (Music/YtCut).

How do I save a video's thumbnail?

Load the video, choose Thumbnail at the top (or Both to get the video too), and press Download thumbnail. YtCut saves the highest-resolution version as a JPG, named after the File name field. It goes to your Downloads folder on Windows and macOS, and to your Gallery on Android.

Clipping is slow on my phone

To cut at the exact second, the clip is re-encoded, which is hard work for a phone. Full videos without a cut download much faster.

Live streams don't work

That's expected. YtCut needs a finished video with a known length.

Is it legal to use YtCut?

The app is a tool. Whether a particular download is allowed depends on the video, the rights holder, the website's terms and the law where you live. Downloading your own videos, or videos with a licence that allows it, is generally fine. Some websites, including YouTube, don't allow downloading in their terms of service.
